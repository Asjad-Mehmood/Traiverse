# Traverse & Level 2.0.1

**Traverse Pro is now Traverse & Level**, the Professional Traverse and Level Adjustment
Software. The program opens the same app as [www.exceltokml.com/traverse](https://www.exceltokml.com/traverse),
in its own window.

- **Log in with your Excel to KML account.** The same account as the website. Every traverse and
  level run you save goes to your account, so it is also in the web app and on your phone.
- **Coordinate traverse.** Station, backsight, the points measured on site and the closing point.
  Bowditch, Transit or Equal adjustment against your precision 1 : N.
- **Levelling.** Rise & Fall or Height of Instrument, arithmetic checks, misclosure against your
  allowable and adjusted RLs.
- **PDF reports, a dashboard, sharing with your team and a traverse simulator.**
- **Works offline** after you open it once with internet; jobs saved offline upload by themselves.
- **Updates itself**, as before.

## Install or update
- **Already have Traverse Pro:** it offers 2.0.1 when it next opens. Press **Yes**; it updates,
  renames itself Traverse & Level and restarts. Jobs you saved as files are not touched.
- **New install (Windows 10 and 11):** download **`TraverseLevel-Setup.exe`** below and run it. It
  installs for your user only, with no administrator rights. If SmartScreen warns about an
  unknown publisher, press **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
  (`TraversePro-Setup.exe` below is the same installer under its old name, for Traverse Pro's
  automatic update.)
- **No install:** open [www.exceltokml.com/traverse/app](https://www.exceltokml.com/traverse/app)
  in any browser.

## Good to know
- **Checking the installer:** `latest.json` below lists its SHA-256. In PowerShell:
  `Get-FileHash .\TraverseLevel-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.
- **The first start needs internet**, to sign you in and fetch the app.
- **Reporting a problem:** [open an issue](https://github.com/Asjad-Mehmood/Traiverse/issues).
