# Traverse & Level 2.1.1

**What's new:** Survey Toolkit can now keep Traverse & Level up to date in the background. Before,
the Toolkit could only update it when you opened it from the Toolkit, because the installer always
opened the program afterwards. The installer now takes a `/NOLAUNCH` option that installs without
opening the program, and the Toolkit uses it.

**What hasn't changed:** everything else. The program's own update check, adjustments, the field
book, least squares, the Qatar National Grid and your saved jobs work exactly as in 2.1.0. Updating
from inside the program still reopens it when it's done.
