# Traverse & Level 2.1.0

**Angles and distances, least squares and the Qatar National Grid.** The program opens the same
app as [www.exceltokml.com/traverse](https://www.exceltokml.com/traverse), so everything below is
also in the web app.

- **Angles & distances field book.** Next to *Site coordinates*, enter what the instrument
  recorded: the angle turned from the backsight (or a bearing) and the distance to each point, in
  DDD.MMSS, decimal degrees or gons, clockwise or anticlockwise. Add **side shots** for detail
  points and tick **Levels** to carry heights through the traverse. Type, paste from Excel or
  import a CSV.
- **More checks.** Besides the precision 1 : N, the angular misclosure (K" x sqrt(n)) and the
  level misclosure (C mm x sqrt(km)) must be within their limits for **Accepted**. The angles are
  balanced and listed with their corrections.
- **Least squares.** A fourth adjustment method for the field book: angles and distances weighted
  by your instrument's standard errors and adjusted together, with residuals, 95% error ellipses
  and the chi-square test.
- **Qatar National Grid (QND95).** The grid scale factor (point scale x height factor) is worked
  out for the job, or typed, and every point and side shot gets its WGS84 latitude and longitude,
  with the same formulas as Excel to KML.
- **Open traverse on a closing point.** An open traverse can now finish on a known point, so it is
  checked and adjusted like a closed one.
- **Exports.** The PDF report adds the field book, angles, side shots, least squares and latitude
  and longitude; the CSV adds levels and latitude and longitude; and a new **Google Earth (KML)**
  button saves the traverse and its side shots.
- **Updated user manual** (41 slides, new section 4) on the
  [product page](https://www.exceltokml.com/traverse), with field book samples to practise on.

## Install or update
- **Already installed:** the program offers 2.1.0 when it next opens. Press **Yes**; it downloads
  the installer, verifies it and restarts on the new version. Your jobs are in your account and
  are not touched.
- **New install (Windows 10 and 11):** download **`TraverseLevel-Setup.exe`** below and run it. It
  installs for your user only, with no administrator rights. If SmartScreen warns about an
  unknown publisher, press **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **No install:** open [www.exceltokml.com/traverse/app](https://www.exceltokml.com/traverse/app)
  in any browser.

## Good to know
- **Checking the installer:** `latest.json` below lists its SHA-256. In PowerShell:
  `Get-FileHash .\TraverseLevel-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.
- **The first start needs internet**, to sign you in and fetch the app.
- **Reporting a problem:** [open an issue](https://github.com/Asjad-Mehmood/Traiverse/issues).
